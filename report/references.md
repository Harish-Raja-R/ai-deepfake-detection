# 📚 Academic References: AI Voice Deepfake Detection

This bibliography compiles credible, peer-reviewed academic literature, benchmark specifications, and foundational software frameworks supporting the AI Voice Deepfake Detection project.

---

## 1. Speech Anti-Spoofing & Deepfake Detection Benchmarks

1. **Todisco, M., Wang, X., Vestman, V., Sahidullah, M., Delgado, H., Nautsch, A., Yamagishi, J., Evans, N., Kinnunen, T., & Lee, K. A. (2019).**  
   *ASVspoof 2019: Future horizons in spoofed and fake audio detection.*  
   In *Proceedings of Interspeech 2019*, Graz, Austria, pp. 1008–1012.  
   DOI: [10.21437/Interspeech.2019-2249](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2019-2249)  
   *Relevance:* Establishes the standard evaluation protocols for voice anti-spoofing, formalizing the Equal Error Rate (EER) and tandem detection metrics across Logical Access (LA) and Physical Access (PA) scenarios.

2. **Yamagishi, J., Wang, X., Todisco, M., Sahidullah, M., Patino, J., Nautsch, A., Delgado, H., Evans, N., Kinnunen, T., & Lee, K. A. (2021).**  
   *ASVspoof 2021: Accelerating progress in spoofed and deepfake speech detection.*  
   In *ASVspoof 2021 Workshop - Edition 4*, pp. 1–8.  
   *Relevance:* Extends audio deepfake evaluation to speech transmitted over lossy VoIP, telephonic codecs, and deepfake speech generated by modern neural vocoders.

3. **Kinnunen, T., Sahidullah, M., Delgado, H., Todisco, M., Evans, N., Yamagishi, J., & Lee, K. A. (2017).**  
   *The ASVspoof 2017 Challenge: Assessing the limits of replay spoofing countermeasure.*  
   In *Proceedings of Interspeech 2017*, Stockholm, Sweden, pp. 2–6.  
   *Relevance:* Foundational benchmark establishing physical and transmission artifact analysis in synthetic and replayed speech.

4. **Frank, J., & Schönherr, L. (2021).**  
   *WaveFake: A Data Set to Facilitate Audio Deepfake Detection.*  
   In *Proceedings of the Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) Track on Datasets and Benchmarks*, 1, pp. 1–12.  
   *Relevance:* Introduces a multi-generator synthetic audio dataset and documents the acoustic fingerprints left behind by MelGAN, HiFi-GAN, and Parallel WaveGAN.

5. **Lavrentyeva, G., Novoselov, S., Tseren, E., Volkova, M., Gorlanov, A., & Kozlov, A. (2019).**  
   *STC Antispoofing Systems for the ASVspoof 2019 Challenge.*  
   In *Proceedings of Interspeech 2019*, Graz, Austria, pp. 1033–1037.  
   *Relevance:* Demonstrates the efficacy of Light Convolutional Neural Networks (LCNN) and Max-Feature-Map (MFM) operations on linear and Mel-frequency spectral representations.

6. **Tak, H., Patino, J., Todisco, M., Nautsch, A., Evans, N., & Larcher, A. (2021).**  
   *End-to-End Spectro-Temporal Graph Attention Networks for Speaker Verification Anti-Spoofing and Speech Deepfake Detection.*  
   In *Proceedings of the IEEE Automatic Speech Recognition and Understanding Workshop (ASRU 2021)*, pp. 1014–1021.  
   *Relevance:* Analyzes graph-based modeling of spectro-temporal relationships to detect voice cloning artifacts.

## 2. Convolutional Neural Networks & Spectrogram Audio Classification

7. **Hershey, S., Chaudhuri, S., Ellis, D. P., Gemmeke, J. F., Jansen, A., Moore, R. C., Plakal, M., Platt, D., Saurous, R. A., Seybold, B., Slaney, M., Weiss, R. J., & Wilson, K. (2017).**  
   *CNN Architectures for Large-Scale Audio Classification.*  
   In *Proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP 2017)*, New Orleans, USA, pp. 131–135.  
   DOI: [10.1109/ICASSP.2017.7952132](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP.2017.7952132)  
   *Relevance:* Validates the transformation of audio waveforms into 2D time-frequency Log-Mel spectrograms for spatial convolution and spatial pooling (VGGish architecture).

8. **Piczak, K. J. (2015).**  
   *Environmental sound classification with convolutional neural networks.*  
   In *Proceedings of the 25th IEEE International Workshop on Machine Learning for Signal Processing (MLSP 2015)*, Boston, USA, pp. 1–6.  
   *Relevance:* Demonstrates that compact 2D CNNs with Mel-scaled spectral filterbanks achieve high classification accuracy while preventing overfitting on moderate audio sample sizes.

9. **Salamon, J., & Bello, J. P. (2017).**  
   *Deep Convolutional Neural Networks and Data Augmentation for Environmental Sound Classification.*  
   In *IEEE Signal Processing Letters*, 24(3), pp. 279–283.  
   *Relevance:* Documents the inductive biases of convolutional filters operating across frequency and time dimensions in audio representation learning.

10. **Lin, M., Chen, Q., & Yan, S. (2014).**  
    *Network In Network.*  
    In *Proceedings of the International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2014)*, Banff, Canada, pp. 1–10.  
    *Relevance:* Introduces Global Average Pooling (GAP) as a parameter-free spatial regularizer that eliminates dense flatten explosion and prevents spatial overfitting.

## 3. Recurrent Neural Networks & Temporal Modeling in Speech

11. **Chung, J., Gulcehre, C., Cho, K., & Bengio, Y. (2014).**  
    *Empirical Evaluation of Gated Recurrent Neural Networks on Sequence Modeling.*  
    In *NIPS 2014 Deep Learning and Representation Learning Workshop*, Montreal, Canada, arXiv:1412.3555.  
    *Relevance:* Formulates the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) with reset and update gates, offering computational efficiency over LSTM while capturing temporal dependencies.

12. **Cho, K., van Merriënboer, B., Gulcehre, C., Bahdanau, D., Bougares, F., Schwenk, H., & Bengio, Y. (2014).**  
    *Learning Phrase Representations using RNN Encoder-Decoder for Statistical Machine Translation.*  
    In *Proceedings of the Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2014)*, Doha, Qatar, pp. 1724–1734.  
    *Relevance:* Foundational formulation of gated recurrent cell dynamics for time-series modeling.

13. **Schuster, M., & Paliwal, K. K. (1997).**  
    *Bidirectional Recurrent Neural Networks.*  
    In *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing*, 45(11), pp. 2673–2681.  
    *Relevance:* Provides the bidirectional recurrence mechanism allowing speech frames to attend to both preceding acoustic history and future prosodic context.

## 4. Generative Speech Synthesis & Neural Vocoders

14. **Oord, A. v. d., Dieleman, S., Zen, H., Simonyan, K., Vinyals, O., Graves, A., Kalchbrenner, N., Senior, A., & Kavukcuoglu, K. (2016).**  
    *WaveNet: A Generative Model for Raw Audio.*  
    In *arXiv preprint arXiv:1609.03499*, pp. 1–15.  
    *Relevance:* First high-fidelity autoregressive neural vocoder; introduced dilated causal convolutions that revolutionized realistic voice generation.

15. **Kong, J., Kim, J., & Bae, J. (2020).**  
    *HiFi-GAN: Generative Adversarial Networks for Efficient and High Fidelity Speech Synthesis.*  
    In *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2020)*, 33, pp. 17022–17033.  
    *Relevance:* State-of-the-art multi-period and multi-scale discriminator GAN vocoder utilized in modern commercial voice cloning platforms (e.g. ElevenLabs, Kokoro).

16. **Shen, J., Pang, R., Weiss, R. J., Schuster, M., Jaitly, N., Yang, Z., Chen, Z., Zhang, Y., Wang, Y., Skerrv-Ryan, R. J., Saurous, R. A., Agiomvrgiannakis, Y., & Wu, Y. (2018).**  
    *Natural TTS Synthesis by Conditioning WaveNet on Mel Spectrogram Predictions.*  
    In *Proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP 2018)*, Calgary, Canada, pp. 4779–4783.  
    *Relevance:* Establishes the Tacotron 2 pipeline converting text to Mel-spectrograms followed by neural vocoder inversion.
